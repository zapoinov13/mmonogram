## Corrected

- Custom seat inserts are assigned black leather explicitly by mesh ID.
- Seat shells and door skins use the contrasting leather finish.
- The shader mapping accepts original names and Three.js-sanitized names.
- Invented gauge artwork is removed from the CAD comparison.
- Dashboard lacquer and the upper leather border have separate roles.
- Rear-facing displays are modeled from the cabin reference, in source coordinates.
- The original CAD steering pad and emblem remain installed.
